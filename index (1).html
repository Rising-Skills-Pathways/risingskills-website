How a year of Rising Techies works

School visits

We introduce girls to what's possible in tech, in Hausa and English.

Rising Skills Tech Fest

A yearly festival in the first term celebrating girls in tech.

Holiday training

After every term, hands-on skills that run on a phone.

Monthly webinar

A live session every month on a career, tool or skill.

Flagship training

We aim to run a month-long intensive in the long vacation, followed by a hackathon.

What guides the work

Fasaha ta kowa ce — technology is everyone's

Three principles shape every session, every video, every conversation.

See yourself in it first

We lead with careers girls can picture themselves in today — a Canva designer, a phone repair technician — before the aspirational ones.

Fit the culture, not fight it

We frame tech careers around what already matters here — working from home, staying near family, earning without leaving.

Build capability, not hype

We don't promise income. We build skills that hold up whether or not results come fast.

Bring Rising Techies to your school or community
