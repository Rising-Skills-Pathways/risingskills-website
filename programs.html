What happens on the ground, and what happens on screen

Two connected programmes — Rising Techies, built for the classroom, and Rising Studio, built for the feed — working toward the same goal from different directions.

Rising Techies · Girls in Tech School Outreach · Sokoto

How the year works

Before any skill, we want every girl to see that tech is for her too — not a boys' club, not out of reach. Then we give her skills she can learn and use on a mobile phone, timed around the school calendar so learning never competes with school.

School visits

During term, we visit public secondary schools to introduce girls to tech careers, in Hausa and English.

Rising Skills Tech Fest

Once a year, in the first term, a festival celebrating girls in tech: talks, demos and role models.

Holiday training

After every school term, hands-on training in phone-based skills girls can start using straight away.

Monthly webinar

Every month, a live session on a tech career, a tool, or a skill worth knowing.

Flagship training & hackathon

In the long vacation (August–September), we aim to run a month-long intensive training, followed by a hackathon.

Holiday training

Careers you can start on a phone

Once a girl can see what's possible, holiday training gives her a first step she can take right away. No laptop needed — many of the girls we meet don't have a computer, so every skill here can be learned, practised and used on a mobile phone. Training is open to any girl who's interested, in school or not.

Skill 01

AI & Prompting

Using AI tools like ChatGPT well: asking clearly, checking the answers, and putting AI to work on real tasks.

Skill 02

Digital Marketing

How businesses reach people online, and how to plan and run simple campaigns from a phone.

Skill 03

Social Media Management

Planning, creating and scheduling content for a page, a cause or a small business.

Skill 04

Virtual Assistance

Organising schedules, emails and documents for others, working remotely.

Skill 05

Digital Products

Creating digital products such as ebooks and templates, and how they are shared online.

Skill 06

Everyday Digital Skills

Zoom, Google Meet and Office tools — the basics for school, work and online learning.

Beyond these skills

Open Pathway

If a participant wants training in something we don't yet offer, they can request it. Rising Techies commits to sourcing that training and we track every request to spot patterns that might one day become a new in-house skill.
